# Grampians V7-9 Boulders — Weekend Trip Shortlist

Compiled from theCrag.com. Grades are V-scale, stars are theCrag's quality rating.

## Access caveats — check before you go

- **Sundial Peak**: the walking track is currently **closed** due to fire damage to the boardwalk (as of mid-2025, expected into 2026). Silverband/Sundial Road access is open but the peak-area boulders may not be reachable. Check Parks Victoria "change of conditions" before planning around this area.
- **Andersens (Hollow Mountain)**: climbing is **banned in Andersens East** under the Greater Gariwerd Landscape Management Plan. Only **West Andersens** (Clicke/Lower Clicke area) remains open. Verify each problem's zone on theCrag before relying on it.

## Best clusters for a weekend trip

- **Mt Stapylton Amphitheatre / Taipan (Trackside Boulders)** and **Hollow Mountain / Andersens** are the tightest, highest-density clusters — multiple ★★★ lines within short walks of each other. Good picks for a base.
- **Sundial Peak** has the best average quality of any area here (several ★★★ at V9) but is currently access-restricted — call ahead.
- **Northern Wonderland Range** is a solid secondary cluster.
- North Grampians, Mt Fox, and Victoria Range are more spread out — good add-ons, not trip anchors.
