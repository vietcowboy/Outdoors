# Grampians V6-14 Boulders — Weekend Trip Shortlist

Compiled from theCrag.com. Grades are V-scale, stars are theCrag's quality rating. Only 2-3 star (quality) problems are listed — 1-star/unrated problems were left out rather than padding the list.

For a filterable, slider-based view of this same data, open [grampians-boulders.html](grampians-boulders.html) in a browser.

## Access caveats — check before you go

- **Sundial Peak**: the walking track is currently **closed** due to fire damage to the boardwalk (as of mid-2025, expected into 2026). Silverband/Sundial Road access is open but the peak-area boulders may not be reachable. Check Parks Victoria "change of conditions" before planning around this area.
- **Andersens (Hollow Mountain)**: climbing is **banned in Andersens East** under the Greater Gariwerd Landscape Management Plan. Only **West Andersens** (Clicke/Lower Clicke area) remains open. Verify each problem's zone on theCrag before relying on it.
- A **voluntary new-route moratorium** applies Grampians-wide — doesn't affect the problems below, but worth knowing if you hear talk of new lines.

## Best clusters for a weekend trip

- **Hollow Mountain / Andersens** and **Mt Stapylton Amphitheatre / Taipan (Trackside Boulders)** are the hard-grade epicenters — dense, high-quality problems across the whole V6-14 range, including nearly everything V11 and up. Best picks for a base if you want variety at a single spot.
- **Sundial Peak** has excellent average quality (multiple ★★★ from V6 to V9) but is currently access-restricted — call ahead.
- **Northern Wonderland Range** is a solid all-round secondary cluster, with quality lines up to V12.
- **Southern Wonderland Range** and **Iskra Crag** each contribute one standout hard problem (V10, V12) but aren't dense clusters on their own.
- North Grampians, Mt Fox/Victoria Range, and Possum Rocks are spread-out add-ons, not trip anchors — Mt Fox is worth a special trip if you're chasing V14 specifically.
