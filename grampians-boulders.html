About this map

Pins mark approximate car-park/trailhead locations, not exact boulder positions (theCrag blocks direct scraping, so these were sourced from park track notes and hiking references).

Several small Victoria Range crags (Mt Fox, Possum Rocks, Buandik, Crag X, Mountain Lion, Eureka Area) share one approximate regional pin.

Tap a pin to filter the list to that area. Selecting an area below (or tapping a pin) drops a red marker on the selected pin.
